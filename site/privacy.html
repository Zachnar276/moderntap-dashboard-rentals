Guest privacy

The short version

When you tap a ModernTap plaque, we count the tap so the home’s owner and manager can see which plaques guests find useful. We don’t know who you are, and we don’t ask.

What we record when you tap a plaque

  • Which plaque was tapped (for example, “Kitchen” at a specific house), and when.
  • Type of device: only “phone,” “tablet” or “computer.” Not the model, not the browser.
  • Which link you pressed on the page (for example, “Choose your dates”), and when.
  • Your answer to “Did this solve it?” if you choose to answer it on a help plaque (“I figured it out” or “I still need help”), and when.

What we don’t record

  • Your name, email, phone number or any account details.
  • Your location. Plaques don’t use GPS, and we don’t look up where you are.
  • Anything you do after you leave this page, including on Twiddy’s booking site.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

The one thing stored on your phone

Your phone keeps a random code (letters and numbers, not linked to you) so that tapping the same plaque twice in a short time counts once. It is only used to avoid double counting and is never saved in our records. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Who sees the numbers

Only the home’s owners and managers, and ModernTap, through a private dashboard that requires signing in. They see totals (like “12 taps on the kitchen plaque this week”), never anything about an individual guest.

Like almost every website, our hosting providers (Netlify and Google) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the service running. We don’t use those logs to identify guests.

Links to other sites

Links like “Choose your dates” or “Guest reviews” take you to other websites, such as Twiddy & Company. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
